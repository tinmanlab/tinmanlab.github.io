Information we collect

When a user signs in with Google, the application may receive basic account information such as name, email address, profile image, and the unique Google account identifier required to create or maintain an account session.

How we use information

We use the information only to authenticate the user, maintain account access, provide app functionality, communicate essential service notices, and respond to support requests.

Data sharing

Personal information is not sold. Information is shared only when necessary to operate the service, comply with legal obligations, protect rights and security, or when a user explicitly asks us to do so.

Data retention

Account information is retained only for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Users may request account deletion by contacting the support email below.

User choices

Users can stop using the service at any time and may revoke Google account access from their Google account permissions page. Requests about access, correction, or deletion may be sent to the support email below.
